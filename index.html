    Before you share a trace.
    How do I get a HAR capture?
    In your browser’s DevTools, open Network, record the action you want to inspect, then use the HAR export option. Browser labels vary. HAR files can contain credentials and private content, even when the browser offers a sanitized export.
    Import a capture you are allowed to inspect. Tracebrief can read HAR 1.2 captures with a log.entries array.
    What is safe to share?
    Tracebrief rebuilds each export from a limited set of numeric and categorized metadata. Original URLs, headers, cookies, query values, payloads, and unknown custom fields are excluded.
    Metadata can still identify activity. Review the result and your organization’s sharing requirements before sending it to a teammate or AI service.
    What does the report leave out?
    Opaque origin and route aliases preserve grouping without revealing names. Dates are shifted to a fixed base. Exports cannot replay original requests.
    It does not diagnose performance causes, inspect encrypted content, or promise universal removal of personal information. Incomplete HAR fields remain unknown.
